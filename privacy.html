Privacy Policy

Last updated 26 November 2026

This policy explains what the Everyday Explained publishing tool (the "Service") does with data. In short, it handles only the channel owner's own TikTok authorisation and collects nothing about anyone else.

What we access

When the channel owner connects their TikTok account, TikTok provides an access token and basic profile information (open ID, display name and avatar), so the Service can confirm the right account is connected. It also provides the posting options TikTok makes available for that account.

How it is used

The token is used only to upload Everyday Explained videos to that same account, as drafts or as posts the owner has approved. Profile information is used only to confirm the connected account.

Storage and sharing

Tokens are stored as encrypted secrets in the owner's private GitHub repository and are never shared, sold or used for advertising. No data about viewers, followers or other TikTok users is collected or stored.

Removing access

The owner can revoke the Service's access at any time in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy, Security, Apps and services, and the stored tokens will then stop working.

Children

The Service is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from anyone.

Contact

Questions can be raised as an issue at github.com/rbuxton03-cmyk/everyday-explained-site.
