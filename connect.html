Connect your TikTok account

Everyday Explained uploads its finished videos to your TikTok drafts. Nothing is posted automatically: you open each draft in the TikTok app, add a sound and post it yourself.

This asks TikTok for two permissions: your basic profile (to confirm the right account is connected) and uploading videos to your drafts.

Continue with TikTok

You can disconnect at any time in TikTok under Settings and privacy, Security, Apps and services.
