Everyday Explained

Short animated videos that explain the science behind everyday things: why your knuckles crack, why ice is slippery, how a toilet flush works.

Find us on TikTok and Instagram as @everydaytasksexplained.

About the publishing tool

Everyday Explained uses a small private tool to upload its own finished videos to its own TikTok account through TikTok's official Content Posting API. The tool is used only by the channel owner. It does not sign in other people, and it does not collect or store data about viewers.

Contact: open an issue at github.com/rbuxton03-cmyk/everyday-explained-site.
